# Data availability and repository policy

This public repository intentionally does not contain the study sequence data.

The influenza sequences and associated metadata used in the thesis were
obtained from GISAID EpiFlu. Their redistribution is governed by the GISAID
Database Access Agreement, so raw sequences, aligned sequences, strain-level
metadata, reconstructed ancestral sequences, and data-derived row-level tables
are not published here.

Users with independently authorised access may place inputs under `data/raw/`
and processed tables under `data/processed/`. These directories are excluded
from version control. Generated tables are written under `results/tables/` and
are excluded for the same reason.

The repository contains the analysis code, the submitted thesis, and selected
research figures illustrating the methods and results. Access to this repository does not
grant access to GISAID data and does not replace the requirement to acknowledge
the originating and submitting laboratories under GISAID's terms.
